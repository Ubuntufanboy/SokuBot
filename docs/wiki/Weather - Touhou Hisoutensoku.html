Weather

From Touhou Hisoutensoku

Introduction

The weather system was introduced in SWR, and was redone for Hisoutensoku. When the match starts, the timer will count up to 99 from 0, and will display a weather forecast. The forecast will change one condition forward in the cycle after a wallslam, groundslam, knockdown, or spellcard usage. Once the timer reaches 99, the displayed weather will immediately take effect, and the timer will begin counting down back to 0. Once the timer reaches 0, the weather effect will end, and a random weather effect will be forecasted. During combat, red crystals will appear. These crystals are automatically collected, and will move the timer toward 0 if there is a weather effect, and move the timer toward 99 if it is clear.

Manipulating Weather

A player can control the weather in variety of ways, if they are aware of the timer. In the period of clear weather, the weather on the indicator will change every time a character is knocked down, wallslammed, or uses a spellcard. Performing one of these actions to change the weather on the indicator to a favorable weather can give an advantage. For example, if player 1 has a health lead over player 2 during clear weather, and the indicator is at "River Mist", using a spellcard when the timer is about to reach 99 will change the indicator to "Typhoon" and the weather will soon activate after. Player 2 will then be at a huge disadvantage, as they will have low health and be unable to block.

Sword of Scarlet Perception

Known as the Weather card, though it's been given a variety of nicknames. Allows the user to take direct control of the weather.

If Sword of Scarlet Perception is used during clear weather, the weather will instantly change to whatever weather type was on the indicator at the time.

If it is used during an active weather, the timer will drop to zero very fast, ending current weather.

The user is invincible for the entire duration of this card's usage.

A weather activated using this card will have its timer start at 99, no matter what the weather's usual max timer is.

Weather Bonus

This is also the cycle weather conditions go through. For example, if the randomly chosen weather during Blue Sky, and a player gets knocked down, the weather will be changed to Hail. The order in which weathers are cycled is shown below:

Sunny Drizzle Cloudy Blue Sky Hail Spring Haze Heavy Fog Snow Sunshower Sprinkle
Aurora Monsoon Scorching Sun Dust Storm Diamond Dust Calm Typhoon River Mist Mountain Vapor Tempest


Sunny

Fly through the sky

Character: Associated with Reimu
Color: White
Effects: Border Escapes are free (but orbs are not regenerated); flight cost is halved, turning radius during flight is greatly decreased.
Normal: ●○○○○→BE→●●●●○
Sunny: ●○○○○→BE→●○○○○
Timer: 100

Drizzle

Spellcards are power!

Character: Associated with Marisa
Color: Blue
Effects:Spellcard damage increases by 25%. When a spellcard is used, the weather timer decreases by 25% of whatever it is currently.
Timer: 100

Cloudy

Spell use becomes easier

Character: Associated with Sakuya
Color: White
Effects: Decreases spellcard cost by 1 (unless already 1); doubles rate at which the spell gauge fills. Weather effect ends if any card is used.
Timer: 100

Blue Sky

Combos become sharper

Character: Associated with Youmu
Color: White
Effects: Skill attacks can be canceled into other skill attacks at the same period when the skill could be canceled into a spell. This does not apply, however, to skills activated from cards. Cannot use same skill twice in a chain. Each consecutive skill cancel in the same chain reduces it's spirit cost by half (1 > 0.5 > 0.25 > 0.12...). Every skill used causes the weather timer to decrease by 10% of whatever it is currently.
Timer: 100

Hail

Spirit attacks become stronger

Character: Associated with Alice
Color: Blue
Effects: Spirit Recovery speed is doubled, increases damage of special moves by 25%. Every special used causes the weather timer to decrease by 10% of whatever it is currently.
Timer: 100

Spring Haze

Physical attacks are ineffective

Character: Associated with Patchouli
Color: White
Effects: Melee attacks can be grazed, at cost of rapid spirit drain (0.25 orbs per frame of graze). Grazing bullets will cause minor spirit drain. When this weather is active, grazing or blocking any attack will produce weather crystals.
Timer: 50

Heavy Fog

Become like a vampire

Character: Associated with Remilia
Color: Red
Effects: Causes Vampirism. 50% of all damage to the opponent (including chip damage) is transferred to the attacker's life bar.
Timer: 100

Snow

Become like a ghost

Character: Associated with Yuyuko
Color: Blue
Effects: Getting hit with or blocking any attack other than spellcards reduces a player's card energy. If the meter for a slot is reduced to zero, the player's currently selected card is lost for the rest of the game. A player will instantly lose a card on hit/block if their hand is full. Losing a card reduces the weather timer by 25% of its current time.
Timer: 100

Sunshower

Defense becomes strange

Character: Associated with Yukari
Color: Red
Effects: Any wrongblocked attack will cause an instant guard crush. Being guard crushed in this weather restores no spirit. Guard crushed orbs recover much faster. Guard crushes produce more red crystals.
Timer: 75

Sprinkle

Special moves go all out

Character: Associated with Suika
Color: Blue
Effects: Causes all skills (special moves) to act as maxed level for the duration of the weather. Using a skill reduces the weather timer by 10% of its current value.
Timer: 100

Tempest

Aerial combat is more effective

Character: Associated with Aya
Color: Blue
Effects: Maximum Airdashes/Flights allowed increased by one. Characters will walk and airdash much faster and farther (flight speed is unaffected).
Timer: 50

Mountain Vapor

Cards become invisible

Character: Associated with Reisen
Color: White
Effects: All cards (both hand and deck) are randomized. The player's hands will be hidden by 5 "?" images, but players keep the same number of cards in hand. Weather effect ends if a card is used. You are considered to have 5 cards when it comes to spellcard consumption, so for example a 5 cost card could be used for 1 cost if it is the front card and you have no other cards.
Timer: 100

River Mist

Distance becomes strange

Character: Associated with Komachi
Color: Blue
Effects: Causes the distance between players to oscillate. Keeping track of the effect while fighting is difficult, so expect many combos and blockstrings to fall apart during this weather.

Timer: 75

Typhoon

Fights become wild

Character: Associated with Iku
Color: Blue
Effects: Characters gain superarmor and will not enter hitstun, and also lose the ability to block. Note that if the weather suddenly clears, even in the middle of a rapidly hitting attack, a player being hit and holding the block direction will immediately go into block at the end.
Timer: 75

Calm

Heals your injuries

Character: Sanae
Color: White
Effects: First character to land a hit will gain a spotlight. That character will regenerate life until the weather ends or until the character with the spotlight is hit which will cause ownership of the healing spotlight to switch. If both players got hit at the same time and the weather activates, the first to tech gains the spotlight. Each time the spotlight falls on someone, the weather timer decreases by 10% of its current time, and the rate of healing increases.
Timer: 100

Diamond Dust

You snooze, you lose

Character: Cirno
Color: Blue
Effects: Groundteching is disabled; knocked down characters take 500 damage from standing up. This damage is not able to KO. The ground damage effect causes the weather timer decreases by 10% of its current time.
Timer: 100

Dust Storm

Counter hits

Character: Meiling
Color: Yellow
Effects: All hits which can normally counterhit will always counter hit. Hitstun time for counterhits also increase an additional 50% compared to a normal counterhits, making some weather-specific combos possible. On this counterhit effect, the weather timer decreases by 25% of its current time.
Timer: 100

Scorching Sun

Incinerate everything

Character: Utsuho
Color: White
Effects: The higher you are in the air, the more your attack power increases (max +35%), but at the cost of HP, which will drain more quickly the higher you are. That lost health is traded for card energy at an increasingly efficient rate the higher you go.
Timer: 100

Monsoon

Bounce off the ground

Character: Suwako
Color: Blue
Effects: Attacks will induce ground/wallslam, which allows for combos similar to "OTG's". Attacks that already have the property of ground/wallslamming will have an exaggerated slam effect. Hitstun time still decreases as combos continue, so some monsoon groundslams can be teched out of.
Timer: 100

Aurora

Anything can happen

Character: Associated with Tenshi
Color: Red
Effects: Causes a random weather above to activate. The actual weather effect must be discovered by the players.
Timer: Same as the randomly selected weather (which makes finding some weathers out easier)
Notes: Determining the actual effect of aurora weather may be tricky, but if you can figure out the effect of Aurora before your opponent, you may be able to use it to your advantage in most cases. A list follows, ranked in order of difficulty in confirmation:
  • Mountain Vapor, River Mist, and Cloudy are immediately apparent to the players.
  • Spring Haze can be confirmed if grazing projectiles or blocking any attack produces weather crystals.
  • Tempest can be confirmed if a dash or attack affected by the speed increase is performed.
  • Scorching Sun can be confirmed by HP loss when character is positioned high.
  • Typhoon and Calm can be confirmed as soon as an attack connects.
  • Sunshower can be confirmed with fast crushed orb recovery or the effect from wrongblocking attacks.
  • Hail can be confirmed with fast spirit recovery.
  • Heavy Fog will be noticeable if substantial damage is dealt.
  • Sunny can be confirmed with free BE/lower cost flight or turning sharply while flying.
  • Snow is confirmed usually only after a card is destroyed due to the effect, as it is difficult to notice the card gauge dropping.
  • Monsoon and Diamond Dust can be confirmed after a knockdown.
  • Sprinkle and Drizzle can only be confirmed by attempting the specials/spells. For some characters, Level 4 skills are visually indistinguishable from the level 0/1 counterparts, making confirming the weather difficult without landing a hit.
  • Blue Sky can only be confirmed by attempting to cancel specials into other specials.
  • Dust Storm is difficult to confirm since it is not simple to distinguish induced counterhits from normal counterhits.
